# CalTRACK

CalTRACK is the foundational methodology from which OpenDSM evolved. CalTRACK itself has been deprecated and is superseded by [OpenDSM](site:), which is now the definitive authority. These pages are retained from the now defunct CalTRACK website to ensure a complete history of OpenDSM (and CalTRACK) for posterity.

- **[History](site:caltrack/history)**

    The origins of the CalTRACK process, from the 2012 CPUC decision through the development of the CalTRACK working group.

- **[Methodology](site:caltrack/methodology)**

    The CalTRACK 2.1 methods specification covering data preparation, model fitting, and savings estimation.

- **[Technical Appendix](site:caltrack/technical_appendix)**

    Detailed testing procedures and empirical analysis supporting the methodological choices in CalTRACK.

- **[Working Group Updates](site:caltrack/working_group_updates)**

    CalTRACK meeting summaries from the CalTRACK 2.0 working group.
